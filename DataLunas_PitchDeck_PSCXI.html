Epidemiological Grounding

The Philippine Anemia Crisis & Diagnostic Desert

Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) is the #1 obstetric killer in the country. Anemia robs mothers of the physiological reserve to survive delivery, yet community diagnostic coverage is practically zero.

🚨 The Pathophysiology Triad
Anemic mothers ($\text{Hb} < 9.0\text{ g/dL}$) face 2.5× to 4.0× higher fatal delivery risk. Myometrial smooth muscle lacks ATP to contract (causing uterine atony in 70–80% of PPH). A normal mother tolerates 500mL blood loss; an anemic mother decompensates into irreversible shock after just 300mL.
🏜️ The Primary Care Diagnostic Gap
Over 200,000 BHWs carry maternal screening on their shoulders with no tools. HemoCue Hb 301 units cost ₱70k–125k + ₱150 per disposable strip, creating chronic stockouts. Subjective physical inspection has poor reliability ($\kappa = 0.20\text{--}0.45$).

Clinical Computer Vision Core

The TingínHB Multi-Site Solution

Point, focus, and capture. TingínHB digitizes physician pallor inspection by evaluating two complementary microvascular sites, executing 100% on-device in under 2 seconds on a budget ₱5,000 Android smartphone.

👁️ Primary Site: Palpebral Conjunctiva
The mucosal lining of the lower eyelid contains zero melanin pigment, guaranteeing uniform optical baseline performance across all Filipino skin complexions (Fitzpatrick III–VI). Adjacent sclera provides an in-frame white balance reference.
💅 Fallback Site: Subungual Fingernail Bed
Non-intrusive alternative when eyelid eversion is impractical (distressed infants 6–24 mos, active conjunctivitis, photophobia). Differential Erythema Index ($\Delta \text{EI}$) cancels periungual melanin bias.
⚡ Inverse-Variance Fusion Engine
When both sites are photographed, the system calculates an uncertainty-weighted composite reading. Discrepancies exceeding $2.0\text{ g/dL}$ trigger an immediate rescreen flag for clinical safety.
Tier Hb Range Action Protocol
GREEN $\text{Hb} \ge 11.0\text{ g/dL}$ Normal Baseline. Continue standard ANC & Iron-Folic Acid.
YELLOW $10.0\text{--}10.9\text{ g/dL}$ Mild Anemia. Review adherence; dietary counseling; follow-up.
ORANGE $7.0\text{--}9.9\text{ g/dL}$ Moderate Anemia. Immediate RHU referral for confirmatory CBC.
RED $\text{Hb} < 7.0\text{ g/dL}$ Severe Anemia. URGENT Hospital Referral — High PPH Death Risk.
📄 One-Tap PhilHealth Konsulta Referral
For Orange/Red cases, TingínHB auto-compiles an encrypted PDF referral containing patient metadata, gestational age, MUAC, and screening confidence intervals. Ready for direct clinical submission with zero manual paperwork.
Architecture & System Capabilities

Six Core Engineering Features

Designed for an entry-level MediaTek Helio G35 or Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 processor in an off-grid barangay station, not a temperature-controlled tertiary hospital laboratory.

📱 13.3 MB INT8 Edge AI
100% on-device quantization. Dual YOLOv8-nano-seg models + MobileNetV3 small regression heads execute in <60ms per site with zero cloud sync.
🎯 Real-Time Quality Gate
Laplacian blur thresholding ($\text{Var} > 120$) and exposure bounding reject out-of-focus or glare-ruined photos before inference occurs.
☀️ Melanin-Agnostic Normalization
In-frame sclera color correction ($k_c = 255 / \overline{\text{Sclera}}_c$) and differential fingernail contrast eliminate external calibration cards.
📊 4-Tier WHO Clinical Triage
Instant color-coded risk stratification mapped to DOH AO 2010-0010 micronutrient directives with visual dual-site confidence bars.
📑 Automated PDF Referral
Instant generation of standardized PhilHealth Konsulta referral forms ready for Bluetooth printing or WhatsApp/SMS relay.
🔒 Offline Encrypted SQLite
Zero-leakage local storage preserves longitudinal maternal hemoglobin trajectories across monthly checkups without cellular connectivity.
