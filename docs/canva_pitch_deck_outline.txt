4. LEFT COLUMN — OPTICAL SENSING MECHANISMS:
   • PRIMARY SITE: Palpebral Conjunctiva (Inner Eyelid)
     - Zero Melanin Bias: Mucosal tissue contains no melanin pigments, guaranteeing identical optical accuracy across all Filipino skin complexions (Fitzpatrick Skin Types III to VI).
     - In-Frame Sclera Calibration: The adjacent white sclera acts as an automatic, patient-specific white-balance reference (kc = 255 / Sclera_mean), eliminating external calibration cards.
   • FALLBACK SITE: Subungual Fingernail Bed
     - Pediatric & Clinical Fallback: Activated when eyelid eversion is impractical (uncooperative infants 6–24 mo, photophobia, eye infections).
     - Differential Erythema Index: (ΔEI = EI_nail - EI_skin) calculates microvascular capillary absorption while subtracting periungual skin melanin.
   • FUSION ENGINE: Inverse-Variance Learned Fusion
     - Combines both site predictions using uncertainty-weighted consensus. Discrepancies > 2.0 g/dL trigger an automatic alert requiring an immediate re-screening session.

   [ORANGE] MODERATE 7.0–9.9 g/dL     URGENT RHU REFERRAL for confirmatory venous CBC

   • 01. 13.3 MB INT8 TFLITE ENGINE (100% Offline Edge Execution)
     Dual quantized YOLOv8-nano segmentation models (~3.2 MB each) and MobileNetV3 regression heads execute in <60 ms per site on entry-level MediaTek Helio G35 chipsets without cellular data.
   • 02. REAL-TIME QUALITY GATE (Laplacian Blur & Exposure Filters)
     Real-time algorithmic gate checks focus sharpness (Laplacian variance > 120), exposure bounds (80 <= Mean <= 200), and eversion posture—rejecting blurred frames before ML inference.
   • 03. MELANIN-AGNOSTIC NORMALIZATION (Autonomous In-Frame Calibration)
     Replaces cumbersome physical calibration cards. Sclera-referenced chromatic balancing and nail-to-skin differential Erythema ratios eliminate bias across Fitzpatrick III–VI skin complexions.
   • 04. 4-TIER WHO RISK STRATIFICATION (Instant Clinical Decision Support)
     Instantly categorizes patients into Green, Yellow, Orange, and Red tiers mapped to DOH AO 2010-0010 micronutrient directives, paired with visual dual-site confidence interval indicators.
   • 05. AUTO KONSULTA REFERRAL PDF (Zero Manual Paperwork Relay)
     Compiles standardized PhilHealth Konsulta referral documentation in one tap, embedding patient gestational age, nutritional MUAC, and screening metrics for seamless primary-to-tertiary clinic handoff.
   • 06. OFFLINE ENCRYPTED SQLITE REGISTRY (Longitudinal Patient Tracking)
     Secure AES-256 local database tracks monthly maternal hemoglobin response to iron supplementation over time without exposing patient identifiable records to unsecured cloud servers.
